# Changelog

Each build is shown as *Version 1.0.0 (Month, Year) Build DDMMYYHHMM*. The build number is the date and time (Australian Eastern time) the build's code was saved.

## 1.0.0

The first version.

- **PocketDeck for Windows** runs in the tray, serves the deck to your Android device over Wi‑Fi, and has an editor to design the deck: keys that open apps, websites, files and folders, press shortcuts, type text, run commands, run Windows actions or switch pages.
- **PocketDeck for Android** shows the deck full screen in any direction, with a rotation lock. It also works in a browser on any device on the same Wi‑Fi.
- **Deck layout:** up to 24 keys a page, in three sizes (Most used, Normal, Less used). Keys of each size form a group; groups stand side by side in landscape and stack when the device is upright, with thin lines between them. The keys arrange themselves to be as big as the screen allows, starting at the top left. Icons cover 65% of every key and app icons stay sharp.
- **Preview matches your device:** the editor's preview takes the size and shape of the connected Android device (upright or sideways, phone or tablet) and follows it when it turns, so it arranges the keys exactly as the device does. Landscape and Portrait show a typical phone.
- **Top bar:** your name and photo, the date and time, and the weather. "Connected for" shows for 10 seconds after a tap.
- **Bottom bar:** the red Lock key (locks the PC), page dots, sound on/off, a volume slider, microphone on/off and the rotation lock. Sound changes show a pop-up on the PC.
- **Look:** four themes, accent colour, background photo, greyscale icons option, digital or analogue clock, key press vibration or sound.
- **Connecting:** PIN or QR code, saved or one-time connections, trusted devices, several PCs, connection history.
- **Privacy and security:** no accounts, no tracking; everything stays on your PC and device. Optional star rating, error reports and update checks only when you choose them.
